Privacy Policy for MapDraw

Effective Date: January 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MapDraw. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our web application. Your privacy is critically important to us.

Core Privacy Principle: MapDraw operates entirely within your web browser (client-side). Your geographic data files (GeoJSON, GPX, KML, KMZ) are processed on your local machine and are never uploaded to, or stored on, our servers.

2. Automatic Location Detection

When you first load the map without a saved location in the URL, your approximate location is automatically determined using the Google Geolocation API to center the map on your region. This provides a better initial experience by showing a relevant area instead of a world view. This does not track or store your location and happens only once on page load.

3. Third-Party Services for Optional Features

To provide optional features like routing, elevation profiles, search, and POI finder, we send the minimum necessary data to specialized third-party services. Your personal files are never sent.

  • Routing: When you calculate a route, the coordinates of your start, end, and via points are sent to your chosen provider (Mapbox or OSRM).
  • Elevation Profiles: When elevation data is already present in your file, it is used directly. Otherwise, the path's coordinates are sent to your chosen elevation provider (Google Maps Elevation API or GeoAdmin API for paths in Switzerland).
  • Search: When you use the search bar, your text query is sent to OpenStreetMap to find locations.
  • POI Finder: Search queries and map bounds are sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass API to find points of interest in the current map view.
  • OpenStreetMap Contributions: When you submit a note or add a missing place, the relevant data (coordinates, description, or map tags) is sent to the OpenStreetMap API on your behalf.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information on how they handle data.

4. Strava Integration

To use the Strava integration, you provide your own personal Strava API credentials (Client ID and Client Secret) from an API application you create in your own Strava account. These keys are kept in memory only and are never saved or transmitted to our servers. When you authorize access, the application fetches your basic profile details and activity history (name, type, distance, time, routes) directly from Strava to your browser. MapDraw's servers never receive or have access to this data. The authentication token is stored temporarily in your browser's sessionStorage and is deleted when you close the browser tab. When you use your own Strava API application with MapDraw, you are subject to Strava's API Agreement and their privacy policy. Strava may monitor and collect usage data related to API calls made through your application.

5. OpenStreetMap Integration

To use the OpenStreetMap contribution features, you sign in with your own OpenStreetMap account via OAuth 2.0. MapDraw never receives your OpenStreetMap password. When you authorize access, the application fetches your username to confirm your identity and submits notes or map contributions on your behalf. All communication happens directly between your browser and OpenStreetMap — MapDraw's servers are never involved. The authentication token is stored in your browser's localStorage so you remain signed in across sessions. You can sign out at any time within the app, or revoke access under "OAuth 2 Authorizations" in your OpenStreetMap account settings.

6. Your Rights and Data Control

All data fetched from third-party services is used solely to provide the application's functionality and is processed entirely in your browser. MapDraw never receives, stores, or has access to your data. You have full control:

  • Strava: All session data is cleared when you close the browser tab. You can delete your Strava API application at any time in your Strava account settings.
  • OpenStreetMap: Sign out within the app to clear your token, or revoke access from your OpenStreetMap account settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
