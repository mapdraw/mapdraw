Local-First

Your files are processed entirely on your local machine and are never uploaded to a server. The application sends minimal data to external services:

  • Initial Map Centering: Approximate location.
  • Routing: Start, end, and via point coordinates.
  • Elevation Profiles: Path coordinates (when not in file).
  • Search: Text queries.
  • POI Finder: Search queries and map bounds.
  • Strava: Authorization and activity requests.
